# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a welcoming place for everyone who builds with Nigerian languages, whatever their experience, background or the language they speak.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect every language and dialect. No variety of Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo, Pidgin or English is lesser than another.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about ethnicity, religion, gender, disability or any other part of who someone is.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual language or imagery.

## Reporting

Email contact@wekoya.tech with what happened and links if you have them. Reports are read by the Wekoya team and kept confidential. We may warn, remove content or ban people from the project, depending on what happened.
